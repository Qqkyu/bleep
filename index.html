Describe the word without saying the forbidden ones. Party game in 22 languages.

22 languages, each with its own deck of cards people there actually know. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard cards, write your own, and share the final scoreboard.

How to play

Play in teams (2–4) or free-for-all, everyone for themselves.

The clue giver gets the others to say the word at the top of the card. Don't say that word yourself, or any of the five words under it, in any form.

In teams, your teammates guess and the other team listens for forbidden words. In free-for-all, everyone guesses and everyone listens.
